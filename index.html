WHAT IS THIS?
This is a topology playground, cellular automaton, and tiny racing game. The track can be a Diamond Ring, a Möbius strip, a figure-eight Möbius, or a trefoil-like loop.
Build patterns with Life
  • Click track squares to toggle them on and off.
  • RUN LIFE evolves the pattern using Game of Life-style rules on the track surface.
  • RANDOM LIFE seeds glowing cells across the current course.
  • RAINBOW ROAD recolors the bright checker squares with a hue gradient that wraps once around each course loop.
Race the topology
  • This is desktop-first: a keyboard and WebGL-capable browser are recommended.
  • Press RACE!, then use the arrow keys to drive.
  • Mossy green cells on your current side give a boost.
  • Dirt-brown cells are lit on the opposite side and act like mud.
  • Red wave cells lit on both sides are hazards.
  • Touching an edge bumps and zaps you.
  • Life keeps running while you race. If you pause Life, randomize cells, or edit a tile during a run, the lap timer resets from your current position and the new setup becomes the run to verify. Rainbow Road is cosmetic and does not reset the lap.
Two-sided surfaces
The game tracks which local side of the surface you are on. On Möbius-style courses, a full trip around can put you on the opposite side. The Diamond Ring also has a second twisted loop that returns you to the other side of the main ring.
Race HUD
  • The minimap shows progress and the current route.
  • Best laps are recorded on the single-loop levels where a lap is well-defined.
  • On the Diamond Ring, drive through the 12 glowing numbered coins (3 inside, 3 on the twisted loop, 3 outside, 3 on the other side of the twist) in any order. The timer is the run time and resets when all coins are collected.
